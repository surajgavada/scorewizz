How Net Run Rate (NRR) is calculated:

NRR = (Total Runs Scored / Total Legal Overs Faced) - (Total Runs Conceded / Total Legal Overs Bowled)

In matches where a team is bowled out before completing their quota of overs, the full quota of overs is used for NRR calculation as per ICC standard rules.
